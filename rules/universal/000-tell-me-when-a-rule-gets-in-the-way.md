# Tell me when a rule gets in the way

When a rule has you do something you think is a bad idea here, whether you followed the rule or not, end the response
with the heading `### A RULE GOT IN THE WAY ###`, then two or three lines: the rule, what it had you do or would have
had you do, and what you think is better.

This is for a rule that gives a bad result in this case, not for a preference of the user's that you would simply do
differently.

**Why:** the user finds a bad rule only by its bad result, and then has to work out which rule caused it. You see it
as it happens. The user, 2026-11-14: "the greatest problem with rules is where they end up overriding your own good
judgment, as in this case. I want such rules gone or fixed."
