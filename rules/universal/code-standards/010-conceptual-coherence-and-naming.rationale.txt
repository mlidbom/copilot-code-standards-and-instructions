## What is behind it

All from June 2026, in the vdgs-live-view project (Vantage). Which models isn't recorded.

- **The `Monitor` case:** a `record struct Monitor(ScreenRect Bounds)`. Asked whether the name and the kind of type
  fitted the concept, the agent answered twice with how the implementation used it. The fix was the rename: it's the
  area a monitor covers, not the device. Behind "When the user remarks on code" and the section on renaming.
- **"Simple, maintainable":** the agent read the words as minimalism and YAGNI. So the standard is stated without the
  word "simple", and the rule says what the user means by the three words.
- **`GlobalHotkeys`:** the agent shortened it to `Hotkeys`, and argued that "Global" was redundant. The user: "make the
  naming universal. not hotkeys, globalHotkeys. Do not fear typing. Readability and being unambiguous is king."

## Taken out, and not to put back

- **Telling the agent not to trust its own reading** ("your own comprehension is a broken gauge"), built on the user's
  "You know everything (compared to me) ... it could probably be named anything and the code would not become THAT
  much harder to understand to you." It had the agent look for ambiguities nobody had met: see
  `007-ubiquitous-language.rationale.txt`. Removed 2026-11-14, a session of Claude Fable 5.2.
- **The force:** "The king of all concerns", "a fatal design error", "you have already lost the thread". Removed the
  same day. The ranking is kept, in the title and in the user's own words.
- **A reader "new to the code", or to the domain:** who the reader is belongs to the rules on comments. In names it
  was the agent's addition, in for one commit.
