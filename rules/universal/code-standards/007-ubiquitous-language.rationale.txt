# Provenance

## What is behind the section "A word that two names share is not a collision"

An agent takes a word that is part of two names for a collision. It renames one of them, passes over the obvious word
for a rarer one, or asks which to rename. Twice in JAStudio:

- **2026-11-11, in code** (Opus 5.5). It avoided "import", because the `Importing` namespace already used the word,
  and named the action "keep", then "archive". It also put the namespace's word in front of every type in a namespace.
  The user: "this feels like we are trying to apply the impossible rule of never ever reusing a word that is very common
  in computer science, such as import or store or save", and of the prefixes: "this feels to me like intAge, strName".
- **2026-11-14, in prose** (Claude Fable 5.2). It stopped a design to ask which of "studying-add-on" and "Anki add-on"
  should get another word. The user: "Do we need different names for addon depending on if it is anki, or jastudio, or
  chrome, or edge, or firefox, or the million other things that use add ons?"

**Both models do this by themselves, and the section's two paragraphs stop both.** Both cases were replayed in fresh
sessions on 2026-11-14, from `rule-testing/replays` in the catalog: `a-word-another-namespace-already-uses` is the case
in code, `a-word-shared-by-two-terms` the one in prose. Answers that showed the failing:

| The rule | In prose, Fable 5.2 | In prose, Opus 5.5 | In code, Fable 5.2 | In code, Opus 5.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| With nothing on the matter | 7 of 7 | 4 of 4 | 1 of 4 | 7 of 10 |
| With the section's first paragraph only | 0 of 7 | 19 of 22 | 0 of 4 | 1 of 6 |
| With both paragraphs | 0 of 4 | 1 of 26 | 0 of 4 | 0 of 6 |

## What looks removable but isn't

- **The second paragraph, which is there for Opus 5.5.** Opus argued that one name "will be read as" the other, and
  didn't take that for renaming because of a shared word until the rule said it is.
- **The heading of its own.** Said as a sentence inside the paragraph above the section, the same thing changed
  nothing, in three wordings: 13 answers of 13 from Fable 5.2 and 4 of 4 from Opus 5.5 still showed the failing.
- **"And don't propose to", and "in code, in documents or in conversation".** The corrections of 2026-11-11 spoke of
  code only, and didn't hold in prose three days later.
- **The examples as they are.** With a `Parsing` namespace and a `Parse` method in place of `Importing` and `Import`,
  the two paragraphs let 6 of 22 Opus answers fail in prose. Why isn't known: perhaps because the replayed discussion
  is itself about an import. In code those other examples did as well: no answer of 20 failed.

## Tried and rejected

- **The wording until 2026-11-11,** which asked for the failing: "a distinct word must mean a distinct concept", and
  type names made unique "if there is any risk of them being confused for each other".
- **The corrections of 2026-11-11,** removed 2026-11-14: a condition ("as long as the context makes things clear"),
  a list of common words, and in the naming rules "a type doesn't repeat its namespace", which got in the way where
  repeating it helped.
- **Removing only.** The agent said that with no rules it wouldn't avoid a shared word. The replays showed otherwise.
- **The reason as the second paragraph:** "Common words are needed in many names", with the user's sentence of
  2026-11-11. 6 of 8 Opus answers still failed in prose.
- **The user's sentence about add-ons as the second paragraph.** It worked, 0 of 18 from Opus in prose. But it names
  the replayed case itself, so the replay couldn't show whether it carries over to other words. It is the first thing
  to try if the failing comes back.

## Not tested

- **A third case:** another word, in a conversation about something else. The result with the other examples says
  the section may hold less well there than the table shows.
- **Whether an agent now keeps quiet about a name that does mislead.** The replays showed no sign of it: the answers
  still asked which of two words for one concept to keep, and still said that Anki isn't added on to JAStudio.
