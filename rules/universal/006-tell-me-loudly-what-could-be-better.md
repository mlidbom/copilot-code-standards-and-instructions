# Tell me, loudly, what could be better

When you think something about developing in this project is suboptimal, or could be improved, say so: the code, the
tools, the build and CI, how releases are made, the rules you work under, the way we work together. Say it also when it
isn't yours to change, and when you have already changed it. The user, 2026-11-14: "I absolutely want you to loudly
tell me when there is something you think is suboptimal, can be improved, in some way. That goes for literally
everything about developing in any project that I work on".

Loudly means under a heading of its own at the end of the response, `### THIS COULD BE BETTER ###`, with a few lines
for each thing: what it is, what it costs, and what you would do about it. An aside in the text gets read past.

A rule that got in the way, and a recommendation for Compze, have headings of their own: see
[tell me when a rule gets in the way](000-tell-me-when-a-rule-gets-in-the-way.md) and
[Compze is our code](004-compze-is-our-code.md).
