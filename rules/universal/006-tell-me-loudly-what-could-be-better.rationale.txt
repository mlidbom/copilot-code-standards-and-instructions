# Provenance

Written 2026-11-14 in Compze, in a session of Claude Fable 5.2.

## What is behind it

Asked what more it would help to own, the agent named four things, and added what it would not ask for: "the release
tooling, the shape of the CI matrix, and the rules themselves. Those are decisions that should stay yours." The user:
"Maybe not ownership, but I absolutely want you to loudly tell me when there is something you think is suboptimal, can
be improved, in some way. That goes for literally everything about developing in any project that I work on in claude
code..."

The same session had an example of what goes unsaid without it. Looking through the user's computer's logs for
something else, the agent found a background download that had failed every three hours for days, judged it beside the
point of the task, and did not mention it.

## What looks removable but isn't

- **"When you think" is a trigger, not a duty to check.** A duty to go through every response for things to improve
  would cost thought on every prompt. The rule on a rule that gets in the way has the same trigger, for the same reason.
- **"Also when it isn't yours to change."** What the agent kept quiet about was exactly what it took to be the user's
  to decide.
- **The heading.** An aside in the text gets read past. The rules on a rule in the way and on Compze have their headings
  for the same reason.

## Not tested

- Whether the heading turns into noise, with small things raised at every turn. If so, look first at a threshold in
  the rule's first sentence, as the rule on a rule that gets in the way has one.
- The heading's wording is the agent's. The user had not seen it when this was prepared for him to commit.
