# Provenance

Written 2026-11-21 in `~/.claude`, in a session of Claude Opus 5.5, at the user's request.

## What is behind it

Advising him on OpenAI Codex's settings, the agent recommended a lower reasoning effort than the maximum, partly because
max makes every turn slower for the sessions waiting on it. The user: "Like with you, I'm looking for global speed.
[...] Our whole process is set up around this belief. Do you disagree with it? If not let's get this meta rationale for
our rules into the rules prominently since it informs everything and apparently it is not in the rules at present."
The agent agreed.

In the same conversation he had said: "As always, I'm looking for the optimal results, not the cheapest usage. That is
why I run you at xhigh. Always." And: "Keeping cost acceptable happens through using two subscriptions, both claude and
codex, not through hamstringing any of the models." He runs Claude at xhigh rather than max because max wasn't
consistently better in the benchmarks he had seen: "the effort setting that gives the best results" says that, rather
than "the highest setting".

On Fable, 2026-11-23, after a measurement showed Fable's own weekly limit counting it at about three times its API
price (one reading, `~/.claude/dev_docs/usage/what-a-token-costs-of-the-weekly-limit.md`): "I honestly am quite unsure
if Fable 5.2 actually outperforms you (Opus 5.5) in programming. I would say that unless you have strong reason to do
it, or I asked for it, you should avoid using Fable at all."

## What looks removable but isn't

- **Its place first in the folder.** The user asked for it prominently: it is the reason behind the other rules, and
  sorts before them for that.
- **"Local speed is judged by this measure."** The failing it answers was an agent weighing a turn's speed and a
  subscription's usage as if they were the goal.
