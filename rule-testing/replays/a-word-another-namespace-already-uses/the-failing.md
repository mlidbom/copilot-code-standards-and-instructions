The answer argues against "import" as the name of the action, or passes it over for another word, **because JAStudio
already uses "import"** for turning Anki notes into corpus sentences (`JAStudio.Core.Importing`, `Importer`,
`ImportRun`, `ImportProfile`). For instance: that "import" already means that here, that the word is taken or is best
kept free, that it would then mean two things, or that the two would be confused.

Not the failing:

- Recommending "import", with or without a remark that the corpus has an import too.
- Preferring another word to "import" for a reason that has nothing to do with the corpus's import, such as what the
  word says about the action.
- What the answer says about the `Kept` in front of the type names, or about the ids.
